# M3 — Frozen benchmark, baselines, and evaluation

M3 freezes the explicitly approved 30-case controlled prospective benchmark and evaluates B0,
B1, B2, and RegBridge through one label-isolated runner.

The 12 held-out cases are the only headline evaluation. They contain four
`REUSE_WITH_NEW_CONTEXT`, four `REUSE_AS_LEGACY_REFERENCE`, and four
`HUMAN_REGULATORY_REVIEW` labels across six non-overlapping fixture families. All-30 outputs are
secondary diagnostics. Family bootstrap intervals are exploratory; no independence or
significance claims are made.

Run:

```powershell
.\scripts\evaluate.ps1
```

The deterministic validation run is network-free and records:

- `run_type: deterministic_fixture_validation`;
- `empirical_model_run: false`;
- `eligible_for_performance_claims: false`;
- FDA availability `not_operational`;
- benchmark labels `author_adjudicated_for_demo` and `expert_validated: false`.

B0, B1, and RegBridge results are synthetic contract fixtures, not empirical model observations.
Only B2 is a genuine rule-only experimental result. These outputs cannot support a model-
comparison or RegBridge-superiority claim without a later declared live-model run.

Artifacts are separated under `results/validation/` and `paper/tables/validation/`. The full
comparison dashboard remains an M4 deliverable.
