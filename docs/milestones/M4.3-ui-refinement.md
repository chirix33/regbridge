### Projection and grouping rules

- Existing placement/metadata/content items carry typed incomplete-inspection,
  intentional-omission and approval qualifications. A standalone inspection item remains
  when there is no substantive item. No three-item cap or string deduplication exists.
- Item badges come from the applicable topic and finding plus material qualifications.
  Full document-level statuses remain in the inventory and document detail.
- Raw metadata, explicit intent, recorded source/target headings and cited applicant
  passages remain distinct from missing intent, partitioning uncertainty and abstention.
- Equivalent observations group only when the system, recommendation, repair, context,
  findings and material qualifications match. Independent findings are retained, including
  several for one document. Shared filenames do not collapse different leaf contexts.
- Stable item IDs include system identity, leaf ID and the recorded finding/topic key.
  The inventory stays one row per leaf ID. Counts name their units and overlap explicitly.
- B2 omission is an intentional capability boundary, not abstention or a request to change
  the baseline algorithm. B0/B1 retain their own input, citations and native explanation.
- Decisions and full repairs are unchanged. Missing cited evidence is distinguished from
  a source-metadata presentation limitation; graph evidence is never substituted.

## Implementation and contract boundary

`ReviewDialog` serve Analyzer and Baselines. The upload view has two alternatives and one
selected-file summary, with generation guards against late sample responses. New editable
setup is always prospective; valid saved metadata intent survives the workspace handoff.

lock, keyboard wrapping and exact focus return. The action queue and inventory have
separate organizations. Evidence, the unchanged graph/text alternatives and execution
records occupy separate disclosures.

The global research note appears only on Analyzer's upload/home step. Scope/provenance and
material result limitations remain in their actual records. Navigation contains Analyzer,
Baselines and About; Evaluation and guided routes still work directly. Local DM Mono and
the supplied SVG/PNG logo remove runtime font dependencies and supply the favicon.

There is no backend API, schema, model profile, regulatory policy or prompt change in this
refinement. Server-owned configuration and historical execution attribution are preserved.
Product configuration errors remain actionable; only the accurate transmission/mode
sentence appears in normal setup. Full attribution remains in technical detail.
